calculateScore(hand: HandModel, totalScore: number, winStreak: number, handResult: HandResult)

Calculates the next score and streak after resolving a hand result.

Wins increase the streak up to MAX_WIN_STREAK and add the hand total multiplied by the resulting streak. Losses reset the streak and subtract the hand total without allowing the score to drop below zero.
